PROJECT SCOPE:Sales trend visualization helps businesses understand how sales performance changes over time. By analyzing monthly or yearly sales trends through charts and graphs, organizations can identify growth patterns, seasonal demand, peak sales periods, and declining performance areas. These visualizations make complex sales data easier to interpret and support better business decision-making.

The scope of sales trend visualization is wide across industries such as retail, e-commerce, banking, healthcare, and manufacturing. It assists managers in forecasting future sales, setting targets, improving marketing strategies, and monitoring overall business performance. Interactive dashboards and graphical representations also help stakeholders quickly compare results and detect opportunities for increasing revenue and customer engagement. Overall, sales trend visualization plays an important role in data-driven business analysis and strategic planning.
